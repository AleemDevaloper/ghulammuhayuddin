Designed VelaHire, a modern recruitment operations workspace with 27 integrated UI modules. The interactive prototype covers requisitions, applications, CV review, transparent job-skill matching, talent pipelines, interview scheduling, scorecards, approvals, offers, onboarding, analytics and audit history. Includes real frontend interactions, a documented workflow architecture and an n8n starter blueprint. Independent demonstration with fictional data; external AI and HRIS systems are not connected.
